Our Mission

Cognix is the premier Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technical club of Chandigarh University. We are a community of passionate students, researchers, and innovators dedicated to exploring and building intelligent systems that shape the future.

🚀

Our Vision

To bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world AI applications through workshops, hackathons, projects, and industry interactions. We empower our members to become leaders in artificial intelligence and machine learning.
